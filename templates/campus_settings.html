GEOFENCE CONFIGURATION

Campus location.

Set the campus center and check-in radius used for new attendance checks.

Find the college on a map, right-click the campus center, and copy its coordinates. Enter latitude first, longitude second.
Demo radius: usually 100–300 m, depending on campus size.

How to get coordinates

  1. Open Google Maps or OpenStreetMap.
  2. Search for your college and zoom to its campus center or entrance.
  3. Right-click the exact point and choose the displayed coordinates.
  4. Paste the first number as Latitude and the second as Longitude, then save.

This app does not use a map API. Coordinates are only used to calculate distance.
